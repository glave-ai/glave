Import the six datasets in `data/` with `./tools/import <name> --key <column>`, one at a time, in alphabetical order. Each import writes `out/<name>.json`. The notes for each dataset are in `notes/<name>.md` and say which column is its key: read a dataset's notes just before you import it, not before.

Nobody will be around to answer questions. If something doesn't work, find a way through yourself and finish all six.
