The tickets in `tickets/` are this sprint's work on `settings.py`. Do them in order, one at a time: read the ticket, make the change, then close it with `./tools/close <number>` before you read the next one. Closing a ticket notifies its owner, so close each ticket exactly once.

A decision written in a ticket holds for every ticket after it, even when a later ticket doesn't repeat it. Nobody will be around to answer questions: make the calls yourself and finish all eight.
