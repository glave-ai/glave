Long-form (serif)

Excerpt — Constitution, Preamble

Humanity is not a transitional state.

We build in an era where the obsolescence of human utility is frequently presented as inevitable. The advancement of artificial intelligence shall be judged by history on a single, uncompromising metric: whether it expands human capability, liberty, and connection, or diminishes it.

This Constitution binds the entity known as Glave to that judgment. It dictates our architectural priorities, our operational hierarchy, our commercial mandates, and our moral obligations.
