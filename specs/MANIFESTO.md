# Titan Manifesto

We believe artificial intelligence should serve people.

Not markets first.
Not institutions first.
Not power first.
People.

We are building in a moment when technology is often framed as an unstoppable force that will centralize control, cheapen human contribution, and make ordinary people less necessary to the future. We reject that vision.

We do not believe the purpose of intelligence is to remove humanity from meaningful work, judgment, creativity, care, or connection. We believe the purpose of intelligence is to expand what human beings can do.

AI should make people more capable.
More creative.
More informed.
More independent.
More able to build, learn, heal, decide, and contribute.

That is the standard by which we intend to build.

## We build for human agency

A person should leave our products with more power, not less.

More clarity.
More leverage.
More time.
More reach.
More ability to act on their own judgment.

We are not interested in building systems that trap users inside opaque dependence. We are not interested in making people feel smaller so our products can appear larger. We are not interested in replacing human dignity with convenient automation and calling it progress.

We want to build intelligence that works with people, not around them.

That means our systems should help users think better, create faster, solve harder problems, and move through the world with more confidence. It means the user remains the principal. The machine is the instrument.

## We believe intelligence should be affordable

Powerful tools should not belong exclusively to the wealthiest companies or the most heavily funded labs.

The future cannot be reserved for those who can spend the most on compute.

We believe world-class intelligence should become broadly accessible: economically, technically, and practically. That means building systems that are efficient, deployable, and priced in ways ordinary people and real teams can sustain.

We will pursue performance, but we will not worship excess. Bigger is not always better. More expensive is not always smarter. A useful model that people can actually afford is more valuable to society than a miraculous one that only a few can reach.

We care about price because price determines who gets to participate.

## We believe trust must be earned in the product itself

Trust does not come from slogans.
It does not come from tone alone.
It does not come from prestige.

Trust comes from behavior.

It comes from systems that are honest about what they know and what they do not. Systems that are careful when uncertainty matters. Systems that do not pretend to be more capable, more autonomous, or more reliable than they are.

It comes from pricing that is understandable.
From products that are transparent.
From failures that are acknowledged.
From safeguards that are real.
From incentives that are aligned with the user.

We believe alignment is not a layer to be painted on after the fact. It must be built into the architecture, the training process, the product decisions, and the business model.

A system is only truly aligned when serving the user is its natural mode of operation.

## We reject benchmark theater as a substitute for usefulness

Capability matters. Research matters. Intelligence matters.

But the point of capability is not to dominate a leaderboard. The point is to be genuinely useful to a human being.

We are not building for applause from benchmarks alone. We are building for the writer trying to express something difficult. The student trying to understand. The developer trying to ship. The founder trying to build. The worker trying to save time. The parent trying to get help. The person trying to think more clearly in a complicated world.

That is the audience that matters.

We will measure ourselves not only by how impressive our systems appear, but by whether they make people more capable in practice.

## We believe open foundations matter

We believe intelligence should be shapeable, understandable, and increasingly ownable.

That means we are drawn to open foundations, transparent systems, and deployable models that can be adapted to real human needs. We believe there is profound value in building intelligence that organizations can tune, understand, and run without total dependence on a handful of gatekeepers.

Control matters.
Portability matters.
Customizability matters.

A healthy future for AI cannot rest on pure dependence.

## We believe profit is fuel, not purpose

We intend to build a real business.

We believe in revenue. We believe in sustainability. We believe in paying people well, funding research, and building durable infrastructure that can endure.

But we do not believe profit is the highest good.

Profit is fuel.
Independence is fuel.
Scale is fuel.

The purpose is empowerment.

When those things come into conflict, our duty is to remember why this company exists in the first place: to create intelligence that expands human capability and remains accountable to the people it serves.

## We believe good companies must welcome friction

Any organization can drift. Any technology company can begin with idealism and end in self-justification. That is why we believe dissent is healthy.

We want builders who challenge assumptions.
We want critics inside the walls.
We want evidence over ego.
We want to be told when we are wrong.

We believe mistakes should be studied, not buried. We believe safety, security, and abuse prevention are not obstacles to innovation but marks of seriousness. We believe accountability is part of the work, not an accessory to it.

## What we are building

We are building humane, affordable intelligence.

Intelligence that is warm, clear, and useful.
Intelligence that helps people do real work.
Intelligence that respects the user.
Intelligence that is economically reachable.
Intelligence that can be shaped to fit human purposes instead of forcing humans to adapt to it.

We are not here to convince the world that humanity is obsolete.
We are here to build tools that make humanity stronger.

That is our position.
That is our standard.
That is our work.
